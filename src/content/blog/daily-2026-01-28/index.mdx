January 28, 2026

Equity indices through Asia were mixed with Tokyo itself mixed while Hong Kong and mainland China stocks jumping over 2.5%. Taiwan and South Korea marked another day of gains up 1.5% and AI and tech names led the gains. China reported to clear its first batch of (legal) Nvidia H200 AI chips for import approving several hundred thousand units during CEO Jensen Huang’s visit.

Last night China resumed its purchases of Canadian canola that are set to load in the next couple months. Importers have also booked shipments for Canola meal for April and June. This move comes a heck of a lot quicker post Canada / China deal than China’s Soybean agreement with the US.

I would call this one a win for Canada at least better than last year where shipments plunged post tariffs. Moving over to Europe, bourses closed in the red despite the strong earnings from ASML (+6). After failing to sustain an early move to the upside, North American stock markets quickly gave back ground and spent much of the rest of Wednesday’s session lingering near the unchanged line.

The major averages eventually ended the day narrowly mixed. The choppy trading continued after the Federal Reserve announced its widely expected decision to leave interest rates unchanged. The Fed said it decided to maintain the target range for the federal funds rate at 3.50 to 3.75 percent following three consecutive quarter point rate cuts.

As with other recent decisions, the choice to leave rates unchanged was not unanimous, as Fed Governors Stephen Miran and Christopher Waller preferred cutting rates by another quarter point. The Fed said the decision to leave rates unchanged came amid elevated uncertainty about the economic outlook. The central bank also said it remains attentive to the risks to both sides of its dual mandate of maximum employment and inflation at the rate of 2 percent over the longer run.

CME Group’s FedWatch Tool suggests investors currently expect the Fed to keep rates on hold until after Fed Chair Jerome Powell steps down in May. With the decision to leave rates unchanged almost universally expected, traders may have been more focused on earnings from several big-name tech companies after the close of trading. Substantial strength was visible among computer hardware stocks, as reflected by the 2.6 percent jump by the NYSE Arca Computer Hardware Index.

The index also reached a new record closing high amid a spike by shares of Seagate Technology, which soared by 19.1 percent after the data storage company reported better than expected fiscal second quarter results. Despite the choppy trading by the broader markets, gold stocks moved sharply higher amid a continued surge by the price of the precious metal, driving the NYSE Arca Gold Bugs Index up by 2.7 percent to a new record closing high. In the bond market, treasuries moved lower early in the session and saw continued weakness after the Fed announcement.

As a result, the yield on the benchmark ten-year note, which moves opposite of its price, climbed 2.8 basis points at 4.251 percent. Trading on Thursday may be impacted by reaction to earnings news from tech giants Microsoft, Meta Platforms and Tesla.
