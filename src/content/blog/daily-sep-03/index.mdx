September 3, 2026

U.S. equities staged a solid rebound on Thursday, with major indexes advancing as Treasury yields eased and technology shares recovered. The S&P 500 rose approximately 1.0–1.1%, the Dow Jones Industrial Average gained around 1.1–1.2%, and the Nasdaq Composite advanced 1.40% to close at 26,584.06. The session marked a constructive recovery after recent pressure, supported by a pullback in bond yields and selective strength in growth-oriented areas.

Sector performance broadened. Industrials, Financials, and Consumer Discretionary led the advance, while Technology participated meaningfully as investors rotated back into growth names. Energy was relatively mixed to softer despite elevated crude prices. Software stood out on the upside following Snowflake’s strong results, with the stock surging more than 20%. Broadcom shares continued to face pressure in the aftermath of its earnings report, reflecting the market’s sensitivity to guidance even after a solid beat. Nvidia gained on reports related to its planned acquisition of the AI platform Hugging Face, adding to residual optimism around the AI infrastructure theme.

The primary catalyst for the equity rebound was the decline in Treasury yields. The 10-year yield fell approximately 5 basis points to around 4.75%, providing relief after recent multi-year highs and helping rate-sensitive sectors. Comments from Federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller, who indicated a preference for holding rates steady absent major inflation surprises, contributed to the reassessment of near-term policy expectations. In currency markets, the Japanese yen strengthened sharply, jumping as much as 2% against the dollar at points and approaching levels last seen after the July coordinated intervention. While the move fueled speculation of official action, Bank of Japan account data indicated no confirmed intervention, with the gains instead attributed to rising bets on a more aggressive BOJ rate hike path. This development added another layer of interest to the global rates and currency backdrop.

Oil prices remained elevated, with Brent trading near $95–$97 and WTI in the low-to-mid $90s at points, hitting fresh multi-week highs earlier in the day. Renewed U.S.-Iran military activity, additional Israeli statements, and still-subdued shipping volumes through the Strait of Hormuz kept the geopolitical risk premium intact. Traffic through the waterway continues to run well below pre-conflict levels, sustaining concerns about potential supply disruptions even as some official comments pointed to ongoing flows under escort.

Overall, the session highlighted the market’s continued sensitivity to the path of interest rates alongside resilient demand for AI-related technology. Strong individual results, such as Snowflake’s, and ongoing corporate activity in the AI space provided fundamental support, while the pullback in yields offered a more constructive backdrop for equities. Elevated energy prices and residual geopolitical uncertainty remain important moderating factors.

We maintain a constructive longer-term view on technology and AI-related equities, supported by resilient demand for advanced semiconductors, data-center infrastructure, and enterprise software platforms. Concentration risk, elevated valuations, residual geopolitical uncertainty around energy supply, and the evolving monetary policy outlook warrant discipline. Our preference remains selective exposure to quality companies with clear visibility into AI-driven revenue growth, genuine pricing power, and strong free-cash-flow generation.

Looking ahead, tomorrow’s August employment report will be a key focus, alongside residual technology earnings and continued developments around the Strait of Hormuz, bond yields, and energy prices. We will monitor these factors closely and keep you updated as conditions evolve.
