April 7, 2026The overnight session started out on a more positive note with Asian equity markets finishing higher across the board. I suppose the market was choosing to ignore the potential downside of President Trump’s 8pm ET Iran deal deadline today in hopes of some type of extension or peace agreement. Sentiment was given a boost after an Axios story circulated noting that President Trump may postpone strikes on Iran’s civil and energy infrastructure.

For the sake of the broader Asian economy let’s hope that Strait of Hormuz opens soon, a few overnight headlines:

Philippines inflation rose to highest in 20 months, with officials blaming the spike in energy costs

Last night’s 30 year JGB auction was poorly supported in Japan, with coverage well below the 12 month average

Australia’s Melbourne Institute Inflation for March jumped to 4.3% YoY from 3.6% prior

China’s growth forecast is getting cut due to the oil shock

Not a lot of great news there…

European markets are less upbeat and traded modestly lower. We saw slightly better than expected PMI data out of the Eurozone today but let’s face it, it really doesn’t matter. It is all about geopolitics at the moment and the longer the conflict in Iran drags on, the more negative the impact on Europe’s economy.

Everyone is hoping for a negotiated settlement between the U.S. and Iran but that might be tough to accomplish. Vice-President JD Vance is supposedly the U.S.’s top negotiator but he’s off in Hungary trying to help pro-Kremlin, EU thorn in the side Viktor Orban win another election. I’m sure the EU is very grateful for the help. North American stocks and bonds moved lower while oil prices rose after the U.S. targeted portions of Iran’s Kharg Island in its latest attacks, and President Trump again threatened utter destruction commencing tonight. Iranian leadership has given no indication they intend to back down and has reportedly cut off all direct contact with the U.S. while threatening chaos for the entire region.

U.S. equity indices remained in the red following a pre-market reversal. The VIX moved up but stayed below 30, suggesting many investors continued to wait and see how this latest act of the conflict plays out.

WTI crude’s spread above Brent oil climbed to $6/barrel amid another move up in energy prices. Apple shares weighed on the NASDAQ following a report of potential engineering issues with their upcoming foldable iPhone. Late in the session, word of a Pakistani proposal for a 2 week ceasefire in exchange for opening the Strait provided a glimmer of hope for a resolution
